# Sealwright Hall

The Hall of the Worshipful Company of Sealwrights stands on a willow island in the Brisk a mile below Summerhythe, reached by a flat ferry pulled along a chain. Above ground it is a college: cloisters, scriptoria, a chapter house with a red-tiled roof, and the gatehouse where the Master lives. Below ground are the Wax Vaults.

## Points of interest

- **The entrance court.** Where every visitor waits to be recognised, and where [[Oswy Hebden]] met the Wardens on day 3.
- **The scriptoria.** Long rooms of sloped desks where apprentices copy contracts and journeymen seal them.
- **The Wax Vaults.** Three levels of vaulted cellars cut into the island's rock, lined with shelves of sealed documents in wax-dipped cases. The lowest level holds Crown documents, and the Crown's copy of the lease.
- **The ink cisterns.** The Hall's own ink is made in stone tanks at the foot of the vaults. Some of it does not stay in the tanks.

## The Wax Vaults

The vaults are dark, dry and warm, because the wax must not crack. Each case is marked with a shelf-mark in the Company's cipher, and [[Clemency Vare]] can find any of them by touch. The lowest level is guarded by smirches: ink that has soaked up so many sealed promises that it crawls. The Sealwrights call them the vaults' own ink, and consider them staff.

## Notable people

The Master, Oswy Hebden. The Under-Archivist, Clemency Vare. The missing apprentice, [[Jory Penwarden]], whose peg still hangs empty in the apprentices' hall.

## GM Notes

The seal-registers in the chapter house record the Marrable silent partnership, sealed three years ago under the name "O. Goldthread". The Master has never had a reason to look.
