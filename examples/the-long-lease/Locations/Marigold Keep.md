# Marigold Keep

The seat of the Crown of Quillet: a square white keep on the hill above Summerhythe, with a hall, a chapel for the Guard, the Chancellery, and a north tower that carries the great crossbow on a turntable. The marigold banners on its walls are repainted every spring and faded by every autumn.

## Points of interest

- **Queen Hesper's hall.** Where [[Queen Hesper]] named the Wardens of the Lease on the evening of the fair.
- **The north tower.** The great crossbow, a lookout's bench, and the best view of the Tally Cliffs in the vale. [[Beatrix Holloway]] was on watch here when the dragon came.
- **The Chancellery.** [[Wystan Pye]]'s offices, and the only warm fire in the Keep.
- **The muniment room.** Where the Crown's charters were kept until the High Water of Lease Year 161 flooded it. The Crown's copy of the lease has been kept at Sealwright Hall ever since.
- **The west gate.** The Keep's quiet way out. [[Emlyn Crewe]] left by it on the evening of day 6.

## Notable people

Queen Hesper, the Chancellor, the knights of the Marigold Guard, and at present [[Sir Ivo Latchford]], who is lodging in the guest wing and practising his lance in the courtyard at dawn.

## GM Notes

The west gate warden told the Wardens about the covered crest and the green lamps. Asked kindly, he will add that the driver wore a boatman's oilskin, not a coachman's coat.
