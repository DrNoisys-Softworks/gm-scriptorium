# The Glad Kettle

A long, low inn on the Summerhythe wharf, named for the enormous copper kettle that hangs, polished and never used, over its door. It has a taproom, a brewhouse, a stable yard, and a long room upstairs where the Wardens of the Lease sleep, argue and keep their charts.

## Points of interest

- **The taproom.** Low beams, a harp with two strings missing, and a settle by the fire that belongs to [[Odda Quarrel]] on quiet nights.
- **The long room.** Over the brewhouse, warm from the mash tuns below, with four beds (two of them slept in), one table and a wall that is slowly filling up with pinned notes about the lease.
- **The attic.** [[Iveth Ammerly]]'s room, where the counterpart lies on a worktable under a warming lamp, its seal softening.
- **The stable loft.** Where [[Kit Farrow]] has slept since he was six, by choice.

## Notable people

[[Mags Threlkeld]] owns and runs it. Her cook, two pot-boys and three cats complete the household.

## GM Notes

The Marrable clerk who calls on the first of the month sits in the taproom for exactly as long as it takes to count Kit's instalment, and listens to everything.
