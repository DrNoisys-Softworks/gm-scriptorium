# Summerhythe

The only town in the Vale of Quillet worth the name: two thousand people, eleven bells, one bridge, and a wharf where the Brisk runs wide and slow enough to land a barge. It was chartered by [[Queen Maudry]] in Lease Year 3 at the old ford, and every Midsummer since, it has held the fair that fills the green from the Wool Exchange to the water.

## Points of interest

- **The Wool Exchange.** The tallest roof in town, with the Great Tenor bell in its tower. Orpiment's claw-marks are now in its lead gutters, and the town council is arguing about whether to repair them or charge admission.
- **The green and the fairground.** Where the Midsummer Fair stands for three days. This year it stood for half of one.
- **Coin Stair.** A steep lane of counting houses above the wharf, including the green door of [[Marrable and Daughters]].
- **The foundry lane.** Where the Quarrel family has cast the town's bells for two hundred years, and where [[Odda Quarrel]] was born.
- **The Founder's statue.** Maudry with one foot on a rock and her sword to the sky, which the Wardens now know is not how it happened.
- **[[The Glad Kettle]].** The inn by the wharf where the Wardens lodge.
- **[[Marigold Keep]].** The royal keep on the hill above the town.

## Notable people

[[Mags Threlkeld]] keeps the Glad Kettle. [[Idony Marrable]] runs the counting house on Coin Stair. Queen Hesper and her Chancellor sit in the Keep.

## Since the dragon came

Trade is brisk and sleep is poor. The bakers have rebuilt two ovens. Children climb the Wool Exchange to look at the claw-marks, and three separate taverns are selling a drink called the Landlady. Queen Hesper has forbidden anyone to shoot at the Tally Cliffs, and nailed her proclamation to the Founder's statue.

## GM Notes

[[Ottoline Pardew]] keeps a locked room at the top of the Marrable counting house, under the roof, that nobody visits, and a house on the Brisk four miles above the town, on the way to the Sallow Hills. Neither is on any public chart.
