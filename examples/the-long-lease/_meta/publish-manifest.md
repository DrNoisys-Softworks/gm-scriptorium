# Publish manifest

This is the player site's fail-closed allowlist. With `publish.mode: player`, a page publishes only if its vault path is ticked under Publishing. Anything unlisted stays off the site. The image filter follows the same list, so only portraits and art used by listed pages are copied.

To publish a page, add one bare line under Publishing: a dash, a ticked box, a space, and the vault-relative path exactly as it is on disk. Put notes in this paragraph, never on an entry line.

PC story companions (`*_Story.md`) are not listed: they publish as part of their PC's page. `Characters/NPCs/Ottoline Pardew.md` is the campaign's withheld antagonist and sits under Excluded on purpose.
