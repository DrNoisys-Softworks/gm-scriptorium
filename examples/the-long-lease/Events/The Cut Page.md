# The Cut Page

Afternoon of day 3, in the lowest level of the Wax Vaults under [[Sealwright Hall]]. After a fight in the dark with the vaults' smirches, the Wardens carried the Crown's lease-case to the reading table at the foot of the vault stairs, and [[Clemency Vare]] broke the Company's seal on it.

## What happened

The first leaf was whole: the parties, the bounds of the vale, the term of three hundred summers. The second leaf was gone. Someone had drawn a razor down the fold, close and straight, and taken it, seal and all. [[Iveth Ammerly]] laid the stub against the grain of the vellum and said the cut was less than a month old. Clemency checked with her own glass and agreed.

Behind the shelf where the case had stood, [[Kit Farrow]] found a folding razor wrapped in a twist of gold embroidery thread. [[Oswy Hebden]], who had come down to watch, sat on the vault stairs and was quiet for a long time.

## Consequences

- Whoever took the leaf knew exactly which case, which shelf and which leaf.
- The apprentice [[Jory Penwarden]], who held keys to the lowest vault, has been missing since ten days before the fair.
- The Crown's copy cannot tell anyone the rent. The Wardens need the Founder's counterpart, which the Hall's own register says was buried with her.
