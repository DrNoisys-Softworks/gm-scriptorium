# The Chorister's Verse

Morning of day 6, at the door of [[Maudry's Barrow]] on the crest of the Sallow Hills. The clay guardian in the niche lifted its head as the Wardens stepped onto the door-stone and sang the first line of a verse the hill people have kept for two and a half centuries.

## What happened

[[Odda Quarrel]] answered with the rest of it, bells and voice together, in the harmony she had worked out at [[Gammer Tansy Holt]]'s fireside the night before. [[Beatrix Holloway]] sang along a beat behind and a tone below. The [[Barrow Chorister]] listened to the whole verse, lowered its head, and stepped back into its niche. The stone door swung inward.

Inside, past a painted passage of shepherds and sheep and one very large yellow shape, the Founder lay on a stone bier with her sword on her breast and a case of amber-sealed vellum under her hands. [[Iveth Ammerly]] read the counterpart aloud by lamplight. When she reached the rent clause, Odda laughed out loud, and nobody told her to be quiet.

## Consequences

- The Wardens know the rent: one song never before sung, made by the vale and given freely.
- The clause headed *Of Default, and of Tender* is still under the Founder's seal.
- Beatrix carries [[Fair Terms]], and has made a promise to its first owner.
