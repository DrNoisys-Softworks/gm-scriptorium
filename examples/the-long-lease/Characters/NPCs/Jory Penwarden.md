# Jory Penwarden

## What the Wardens Know

Jory is nineteen, a third-year apprentice Sealwright with a good hand and a habit of whistling in the vaults, which is forbidden. He held keys to the lower Wax Vaults. Ten days before the Midsummer Fair he did not come to morning roll, and nobody at [[Sealwright Hall]] has seen him since.

His peg in the apprentices' hall still has his name on it and nothing hanging from it. [[Kit Farrow]] knew him slightly, from two evenings at [[The Glad Kettle|the Glad Kettle]], and liked him. In the vault behind the lease-case the Wardens found a razor wrapped in a twist of gold embroidery thread. Nobody has yet said out loud that the two things are connected.

## Who He Is

The son of a ferryman from the lower Brisk, apprenticed on a scholarship he did not expect to win. He owes nobody money that anyone knows about. He sent his mother a crown at midsummer, which is more than an apprentice sees in a season: the Company feeds and houses its apprentices and pays them in pennies.

## GM Notes

Jory took a retainer in Lease Year 299 to learn which shelf held the lease, and cut the page on the promise of a year's wages and a place as a clerk in a counting house nobody would name. He re-pressed the Company's seal on the case with an apprentice's practice stamp; the impression is a hair too shallow, and Clemency will see it if she looks again. He is alive, frightened, and being kept at a house on the Brisk above the town by the woman who paid him, who signs her letters with a gold thread. If the Wardens find him, he will tell them everything he knows, which is less than they will hope. He knows the thread, not the name.
