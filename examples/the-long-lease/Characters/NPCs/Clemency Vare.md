# Clemency Vare

Clemency walks the Wax Vaults with a hooded lamp in one hand and a brass rod in the other, tapping the shelves as she passes to hear whether anything has moved inside them. She talks while she walks and does not look back to see whether you are keeping up.

## Who she is

At fourteen Clemency was [[Iveth Ammerly]]'s apprentice, the year Iveth was expelled. She stayed on under [[Oswy Hebden]], passed every examination at the first sitting, and was made Under-Archivist at thirty, the youngest in the Company's history. She has never forgiven Iveth for leaving, and has never said so, which Iveth has noticed.

## What she wants

Her apprentice back, and an explanation from him. [[Jory Penwarden]] had keys to the lower vaults because Clemency asked for them on his behalf and the Master signed for them. If he cut the page, it was with keys she vouched for, and she wants to hear why from him before she hears it from anyone else.

## Dealings with the Wardens

She guided the Wardens into the Wax Vaults on day 3, fought the smirches beside them with the brass rod and a jar of lamp-oil, and opened the lease-case herself. When she saw the cut, she checked Iveth's estimate of its age against the vellum grain with her own glass, and said "Yes" without looking up.

## GM Notes

Clemency is the Wardens' best ally inside the Hall and she will become a friend if they bring Jory back alive. She has one piece of information she has not shared: Jory asked her, two months ago, whether a lease could be paid by someone other than the tenant. She laughed at him, and since the cut she has gone back over that conversation every night.
