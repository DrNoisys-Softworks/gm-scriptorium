# Ottoline Pardew

The campaign's antagonist. The table has never heard her name. This page is `withheld: true`, stays under Excluded in the publish manifest, and exists so the leak checks have something to protect.

## Who she is

A merchant's widow from downriver who made a shipping fortune into a lending one, and who has spent thirty years buying other people's debts at a discount. She signs private letters with a knot of gold thread instead of a name, which is where "Mistress Goldthread" comes from.

## The plan

Three years ago she read an old copy of the Founder's charter in a dead collector's library and found a reference to the lease's assignment clause: whoever tenders the rent holds the lease. She bought into the Marrable house as a silent partner, lent the Crown money it could not repay, paid [[Jory Penwarden]] to cut the rent clause out of the Crown's copy, and read it. When she learned that the rent is a song, she hired the best composer in the vale to write it.

On the Lammas moon, if the Crown cannot pay, she means to climb to the signing stone with [[Emlyn Crewe]] and tender the rent herself. Orpiment will honour the clause. The vale will pass to Ottoline for three hundred summers without a sword being drawn, and she will hold every debt in it.

When the Wardens read the seal-register entry "O. Goldthread", or hear the name from Jory's captors, this page comes off `withheld` and its path moves from Excluded to Publishing in the manifest.

## GM Notes

She is patient, courteous, and never in the room. Her agents are the Marrable clerk, a boatman called Dab Kitchin, and the carriage with green lamps that Kit saw from the hills. She will not be met until the Wardens have earned it, and not before session six. If the Wardens pay the rent first, she has lost, and she will try to buy the Wardens' song instead.
