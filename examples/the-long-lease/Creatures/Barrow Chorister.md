# Barrow Chorister

A life-sized figure of fired red clay in the shape of a robed singer, hands folded over a clay lyre, face worn smooth by two and a half centuries of weather. It stands in a niche beside the door of [[Maudry's Barrow]]. When anyone sets foot on the door-stone, it lifts its head and sings.

## Habits

The chorister was made by the first Sealwrights, at the Founder's own request, to keep her rest. Its test is courtesy. It sings the first line of the barrow-verse and waits. Answer it, and it steps aside and lets you pass with your companions. Fail to answer, and it sings again, differently, and the second song puts people to sleep. It will not harm a sleeper. It will harm someone who strikes it.

## At the table

Brief stats for fifth edition: AC 15, HP 60, speed 20 feet, Strength 16, Dexterity 8, Constitution 16. Immune to poison and psychic damage. Vulnerable to thunder.

Fought straight, it is a CR 3 construct, 700 XP, a Low fight for four level 3 characters whose real danger is the lullaby: one bad round can drop most of a party. Because it never strikes an Unconscious creature, that danger costs time and dignity rather than lives. The Wardens earned the XP by the verse. The Wardens did not fight it. On day 6 [[Odda Quarrel]] answered its verse, with [[Beatrix Holloway]] singing along a beat behind, and it stepped aside.

## GM Notes

The chorister's song is the only surviving copy of three of Maudry's own tunes. A bard who listened to it long enough could learn them, and a song for a dragon might begin there.
