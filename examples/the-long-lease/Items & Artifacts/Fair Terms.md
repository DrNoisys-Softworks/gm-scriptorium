# Fair Terms

The Founder's sword: a plain longsword with a steel hilt wrapped in green leather and a single marigold engraved at the base of the blade. It is shorter than the statue in the square suggests. [[Queen Maudry]] carried it to the signing on the Tally Cliffs and, according to the lease itself, never drew it there.

## Properties

- A longsword of old, plain steel that has never needed sharpening.
- Once per long rest, the wielder can speak its name and cast *zone of truth* (save DC 13) centred on the blade.
- Its edge will not bite a creature that the wielder has given their word not to harm. It gives no warning. It simply turns in the hand, and the attack misses.

## History

The Founder's household called it Fair Terms because she named it herself, the day she came down from the cliffs with a lease in place of a victory. It lay on her breast in [[Maudry's Barrow]] for 256 years. [[Beatrix Holloway]] took it on day 6, after asking the Founder's permission aloud, and has carried it since.

## GM Notes

The third property matters more than it looks. In the square on day 1 Beatrix promised Orpiment that she would not raise a weapon against her while the lease stands, and her promises are listed word for word on her page. If she ever swings this sword at the dragon, the attack misses: no smite, no mastery effect, nothing.
